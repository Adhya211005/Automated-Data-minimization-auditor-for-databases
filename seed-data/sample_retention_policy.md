# ACME Corp — Data Retention Policy

_Document version 4.1 — reviewed every 12 months. Last review: 2026-02-01._

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how long ACME Corp retains categories of personal and
operational data, in line with GDPR Article 5(1)(e) (storage limitation) and
the DPDP Act. Data that is no longer required for its stated purpose must be
deleted or anonymised.

## 2. Retention periods

### 2.1 Financial and transaction records

Order and transaction records, including invoices and payment details, **must
be retained for 7 years** from the date of the transaction to meet tax and
accounting obligations. This includes shipping and billing information
associated with an order.

### 2.2 Customer support

Customer support tickets and related correspondence **should be deleted after
1 year** from the date the ticket is closed. Ticket satisfaction survey
responses are kept for 6 months only.

### 2.3 User accounts

User account records and associated profile information are retained for
**3 years** after the account is last active. Accounts inactive for longer
than this period are scheduled for deletion.

### 2.4 Authentication and security logs

Login history, including source IP addresses and session timestamps, is
retained for **90 days** for security monitoring, after which it is purged.

### 2.5 Marketing

Marketing consent records are retained for **2 years** unless the customer
renews their consent, in which case the period restarts. Records of consent
withdrawal are kept for 3 years as proof of compliance.

### 2.6 Analytics and demographics

Aggregated analytics data has no fixed retention limit. Demographic
attributes collected for reporting (such as gender and age band) are held for
a maximum of **18 months**.

### 2.7 All other personal data

Any category of personal data not listed above is retained for no longer than
**2 years** by default.

## 3. Related timescales (not retention periods)

- Data subject access requests must be fulfilled within **30 days** of receipt.
- Personal data breaches are reported to the supervisory authority within
  **72 hours**.
- New staff must complete data-protection training within **14 days** of
  their start date.
- Backups are rotated on a **35 day** cycle; this does not extend the
  retention periods above.
